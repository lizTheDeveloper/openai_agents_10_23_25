# Empirical Tests and Limitations

## Empirical Tests
- Various proposals and experiments have been undertaken to look for evidence that our universe has holographic properties.
- **Fermilab's Holometer:** This experiment was designed to detect "holographic noise" — tiny quantum fluctuations of space that might be expected if spacetime were fundamentally holographic. As of now, the experiment has reported no positive signals, putting constraints on some models.
- **Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB):** Some analyses of the CMB (the afterglow of the Big Bang) have argued that tiny fluctuations might reveal a holographic structure to the universe. Evidence is suggestive, but controversial and not confirmed.

## Limitations and Criticisms
- **AdS/CFT is not our universe:** The AdS/CFT correspondence is best understood in spacetimes with negative curvature (AdS), not our universe's observed (approximately flat or positively curved) geometry.
- **No direct experimental evidence:** While theoretically compelling, the holographic principle has no definitive, widely-accepted experimental confirmation so far.
- **Ongoing debate:** Both the theoretical frameworks and potential tests are under active investigation in theoretical and experimental physics.
