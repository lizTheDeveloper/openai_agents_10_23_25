# AdS/CFT Correspondence

## Definition
The **AdS/CFT correspondence** (Anti-de Sitter/Conformal Field Theory correspondence) is a theoretical duality that posits an exact equivalence between a theory of gravity in a (d+1)-dimensional Anti-de Sitter (AdS) spacetime and a conformal field theory (CFT) defined on its d-dimensional boundary. It provides the most concrete realization of the holographic principle in string theory.

## Historical Background
- Proposed by Juan Maldacena in 1997 (published in 1998).
- First major evidence and checks by Gubser, Klebanov, Polyakov, and Witten in late 1990s.

## Significance
- Allows study of quantum gravity using quantum field theory (and vice versa).
- Known as a strong/weak duality: difficult calculations in one theory can be translated into easier calculations in the dual theory.
- Used to study black holes, quark-gluon plasma, and other systems.

## Key Quote and Source
- “The most well-studied example is the duality between type IIB string theory on AdS₅ × S⁵ and N = 4 supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory on the 4-dimensional boundary.” ([Wikipedia: AdS/CFT correspondence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdS/CFT_correspondence))
